# 1. Business Overview

AstraQuant is a financial technology platform designed to provide investors with intelligent market analysis, portfolio management capabilities, and AI-powered investment insights.

The platform will initially focus on three major markets:

- Egyptian Exchange (EGX)
- Qatar Stock Exchange (QSE)
- United States Markets (NYSE / NASDAQ)


---

# 2. Business Objectives

The main business objectives of AstraQuant are:

## BO-001: Centralized Investment Platform

Provide a unified platform where users can access market data, company analysis, portfolio tracking, and investment insights.

---

## BO-002: Improve Investment Decisions

Help investors make better decisions through:

- Financial analysis.
- Technical indicators.
- Market trends.
- Artificial intelligence recommendations.

---

## BO-003: Reduce Research Time

Reduce the effort required by investors to collect and analyze information from multiple sources.

---

## BO-004: Create Multiple Revenue Streams

Support a hybrid business model:

- Free users.
- Professional subscriptions.
- Enterprise solutions.
- API services.

# 4. Business Requirements

## BR-001 Market Data Access

The system shall provide users with access to stock market information from supported markets.

---

## BR-002 Company Information

The system shall provide detailed information about listed companies including:

- Company profile.
- Sector.
- Industry.
- Financial information.
- Historical prices.


---

## BR-003 Portfolio Management

The system shall allow users to:

- Create portfolios.
- Add transactions.
- Monitor performance.
- Calculate profit and loss.


---

## BR-004 Watchlist Management

The system shall allow users to:

- Add companies to watchlists.
- Monitor price changes.
- Receive alerts.


---

## BR-005 Investment Analytics

The system shall provide:

- Technical analysis.
- Financial ratios.
- Company comparison.
- Market indicators.


---

## BR-006 AI Investment Assistant

The system shall provide AI capabilities including:

- Stock analysis summaries.
- Market insights.
- Personalized recommendations.


---

## BR-007 Subscription Management

The system shall support multiple subscription plans:

- Free.
- Pro.
- Professional.
- Enterprise.


---

# 5. Business Rules

## Rule-001 Subscription Access

Users can access features based on their subscription level.

---

## Rule-002 Data Access

Premium market data features may require a paid subscription.

---

## Rule-003 Portfolio Ownership

Users can only access and manage their own portfolios.

---

## Rule-004 Enterprise Isolation

Enterprise customers must have isolated data environments.


---

# 6. MVP Scope

The first release will include:

## Included

- User registration and login.
- Market overview.
- Stock search.
- Company profiles.
- Historical prices.
- Watchlist.
- Basic portfolio.
- Basic analytics.


## Not Included Initially

- Automated trading.
- Direct broker integration.
- Advanced algorithmic trading.
- Institutional trading systems.


---

# 7. Success Criteria

The MVP will be considered successful when:

- Users can analyze stocks.
- Users can create portfolios.
- Users can track market movements.
- System supports multiple markets.
- Platform architecture allows future expansion.


---

# 8. Risks

## Data Availability Risk

Dependence on external market data providers.

## Regulatory Risk

Financial platforms require compliance with local regulations.

## Scalability Risk

Large market data volumes require scalable architecture.
