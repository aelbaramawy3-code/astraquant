# AstraQuant Vision Document

**Version:** 1.0

**Status:** Draft

---

# 1. Project Overview

AstraQuant is an AI-powered investment intelligence platform designed to help investors analyze financial markets, monitor portfolios, discover investment opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

The platform will initially support:

- Egyptian Exchange (EGX)
- Qatar Stock Exchange (QSE)
- United States Markets (NYSE / NASDAQ)

---

# 2. Vision Statement

To build a smart investment ecosystem that combines financial data, analytics, and artificial intelligence to simplify investment decisions for individual investors, professionals, and financial institutions.

---

# 3. Problem Statement

Investors face several challenges:

- Difficulty collecting information from multiple sources.
- Lack of unified analysis across different markets.
- Limited understanding of financial indicators.
- Time-consuming manual research.
- Difficulty monitoring portfolio performance.
- Lack of personalized investment insights.

AstraQuant aims to solve these challenges through a unified intelligent platform.
